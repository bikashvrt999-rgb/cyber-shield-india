Indian Cyber Help

Suspect financial cyber fraud?

  • Call 1930 promptly for financial cyber fraud.
  • Use the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.
  • Contact your bank through its official app or verified number.
  • Never share an OTP, PIN or password.
🧠

Stay Safe Online

  • Never pay a fee just because a stranger promises a job or reward.
  • Verify government schemes through official government websites.
  • Do not install unknown APK files sent by strangers.
  • Never install a screen-sharing app at the request of an unknown caller.
  • Use unique passwords and enable two-factor authentication.

Important: This is a rule-based educational prototype, not an AI system or a professional security scanner. Its results may be wrong. A low score does not mean a message or website is safe.
