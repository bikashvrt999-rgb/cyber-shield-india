Indian Cyber Emergency Help

If you suspect financial cyber fraud:

  • Call 1930 promptly for financial cyber fraud.
  • Report through the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.
  • Contact your bank using its official app or verified number.
  • Never share OTPs, PINs or passwords.

Cyber Safety Tips

  • Never share an OTP, PIN or password with a caller.
  • Verify job offers through the employer's official website.
  • Do not install unknown APK files sent by strangers.
  • Never transfer money to receive a promised prize.
  • Use unique passwords and enable two-factor authentication.

About this project

This is a free, rule-based cyber awareness prototype. It does not connect to a live threat database and is not a professional security scanner. Results may be wrong. A low score does not mean a message or link is safe.
